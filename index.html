About Hermes

Hermes is a personal automation assistant application developed by Tegar Andriyansyah. It connects to the developer's own Google account through Google APIs to help manage calendars, documents, spreadsheets, and email as part of personal productivity workflows.

Hermes is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered as a public service and no third-party accounts are supported.

What Hermes Does

  • Reads and manages Google Calendar events for the account owner
  • Creates and edits Google Docs, Sheets, and Drive files on request
  • Handles personal email automation (Gmail) for the account owner
  • Performs personal task automation and research workflows
