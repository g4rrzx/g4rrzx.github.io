Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to personal applications and automation tools developed and operated by Tegar Andriyansyah ("I", "my", "me"), including the personal assistant application referred to as "Hermes Agent".

1. Information Collected

My personal applications may access the following data through Google APIs, strictly with the account owner's authorization (OAuth 2.0):

  • Google Calendar events (to read and manage the owner's personal schedule)
  • Google Drive files and Google Docs/Sheets (to create and manage documents at the owner's request)
  • Google Contacts (read-only, for personal contact lookups)
  • Gmail messages (read/send/modify, for personal email automation)

2. How Information Is Used

All accessed data is used exclusively by and for the account owner (the individual who authorized access). Data is used solely to provide personal automation functionality: managing calendars, documents, contacts, and email at the owner's explicit request.

3. Data Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose. No data is made available to other users or organizations.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's private device with restricted file permissions. Access tokens are refreshed securely via Google's OAuth 2.0 endpoints. No copies of the owner's Google data are stored on external servers beyond what is necessary for the requested operation at the time it is performed.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately and permanently terminates the application's ability to access Google data. Locally stored tokens can be deleted at any time by the owner.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
